    What helps your heart

    Small habits, kept up for years, count for more than any single test result.

    Eat more plants
    Fill your plate with vegetables, fruit, whole grains, beans and nuts. Go easy on salt, sugary drinks and processed meat.
    Move most days
    Aim for about 150 minutes of brisk walking, cycling or similar activity each week.
    Sleep and unwind
    Most adults need seven to nine hours of sleep. Poor sleep and constant stress both push blood pressure up.
    Know your numbers
    High blood pressure, cholesterol and blood sugar rarely cause symptoms, so measure them regularly. If you smoke, quitting is the biggest single change you can make.

    The model is a k-nearest-neighbours classifier. To score you, it finds the {{ info.neighbors }} patients in the dataset whose answers are closest to yours and counts how many of them had heart disease.
